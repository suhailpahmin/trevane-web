Trevane — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Trevane is a medication reminder and tracking app. This policy describes what information Trevane collects, how it is used, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account information — if you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email. You can also use Trevane anonymously without an account.
  • Medication data — medication names, dose schedules, and your log of taken/missed doses. This is the core data Trevane needs to function.
  • Caregiver links — if you invite a caregiver or accept an invite, we store that link so the connected account can see dose status and low-stock flags. Caregivers never see your exact medication quantities, your full medication list, or your history unless you choose to share more.

How we use it

Medication and dose data is used only to run the app: showing your schedule, sending reminders, tracking inventory, and sharing the limited status information you authorize with a linked caregiver.

We do not use your medication data for advertising, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

No advertising, no ad trackers

Trevane contains no advertising SDKs and no advertising trackers. We do not target ads based on your health information, because we don't show ads at all.

Third parties we use to run the app

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Your account and medication data is stored there, encrypted in transit.
  • Apple / Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with either, authentication is handled by Apple or Google directly; Trevane never sees your password.
  • RxTerms (U.S. National Library of Medicine) — when you search for a medication name, Trevane queries this public, free medication-name lookup service to suggest real drug names. No account or personal data is sent with this lookup, only the text you type.
  • Sentry — used only to catch crashes and technical errors so we can fix them. This is diagnostic tooling, not advertising or tracking: no medication names, no account identity, and no health data are ever sent to it. Network request details (including any URLs that could contain account identifiers) are stripped before anything leaves your device.

We do not share your medication or health data with any other third party, and we do not sell it.

Your choices

  • You can use Trevane anonymously, without creating an account.
  • You control who you invite as a caregiver, and you can revoke that link at any time.
  • You can delete your account and data from within the app's Settings.

Medical disclaimer

Trevane is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not calculate or recommend doses. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist before making medical decisions about your medications.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date above will change and, for material changes, we will notify users in the app.
